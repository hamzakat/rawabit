On June 6, 2025, demonstrators began to assemble outside of the Metropolitan Detention Center in Los Angeles in response to accelerated federal raids targeting suspected undocumented immigrants.

The June 6, 2025 assembly catalyzed a series of mass protests across the country to denounce U.S. immigration policy.

This visual investigation spotlights three novel weapons.

The three weapons are scattershot impact projectiles, powder blast dispersion rounds, and chemical obscurants.

The analysis covers immigration protests from June 2025 to the present.

Scattershot impact projectiles used by the Los Angeles Police Department are described as tools of collective punishment on assemblies rarely seen in the United States.

Scattershot impact projectiles can be directly linked to the nearly 100 journalists shot with less-lethal weapons during the anti-ICE protests in Los Angeles from the summer of 2025.

Powder blast dispersion rounds are often referred to by the trade name “Muzzle Blast” rounds.

Powder blast dispersion rounds were once largely confined to correctional facilities.

Powder blast dispersion rounds have recently been used by the United States Border Patrol on the streets.

Video footage shows powder blast dispersion rounds being direct fired at protesters’ faces at very close range.

Chemical obscurants are more commonly known as “smoke grenades.”

The use of chemical obscurants has a widespread misinformation problem about both identification and health impacts.

Manufacturers advertise obscurants on the civilian market as aids to crowd-control.

Manufacturers also advertise obscurants to intimidate through the psychological effect of smoke or to allegedly heighten the negative effects of tear gas.
